# Customer Feedback Triage

Triage order:

1. Identify the feedback category.
2. Confirm the material is synthetic or de-identified and customer-safe.
3. Escalate any PHI, raw DICOM, real clinical report, real customer name, real
   hospital name, clinical validation, regulatory approval, or certification
   request.
4. Convert accepted feedback into documentation, report-template, delivery, or
   handoff tasks.

Out-of-scope requests are rejected or routed outside the technical review
workflow.
