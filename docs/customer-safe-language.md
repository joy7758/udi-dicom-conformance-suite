# Customer Safe Language

Use:

- "synthetic pilot review"
- "evidence consistency review"
- "delivery package check"
- "redaction check status"
- "review/report output"
- "not clinical validation"
- "not regulatory approval"
- "not certification"

Avoid:

- statements that imply clinical validation
- statements that imply regulatory approval
- statements that imply certification
- statements that imply raw DICOM delivery
- statements that imply PHI processing

## v0.5 Required Language

Use: "synthetic customer dry run", "technical review", "customer-safe report",
"service language audit", and "handoff checklist".

Avoid: diagnosis, regulatory clearance, certification, compliance certificate,
hospital deployment promise, real customer approval, or real sample acceptance
without separate review gates.
