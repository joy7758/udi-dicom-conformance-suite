# Partner Feedback Triage

Triage order:

1. Confirm the feedback category.
2. Confirm the report references partner-safe or synthetic material.
3. Reject or escalate requests involving PHI, real samples, confidential vendor
   rules, clinical validation, regulatory approval, or certification.
4. If accepted, map the feedback to partner pack documentation, expected output
   review, registry fixture clarification, or boundary documentation.

Private cases remain internal and are not copied into partner-facing outputs.
