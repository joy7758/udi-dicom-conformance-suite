# Private Asset Governance v0.6

Private suite assets are governed as internal synthetic conformance and partner
review infrastructure.

Rules:

- Private cases do not enter `partner-pack/generated/`.
- Partner dry run material is summary-only and partner-safe.
- Release assets contain no raw DICOM files and no PHI.
- Vendor rules must remain generic and must not encode real confidential rules.
- Public paper support may mention that the private suite exists as controlled
  partner-review infrastructure, but it must not disclose private case content.
