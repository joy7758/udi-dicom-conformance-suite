# v0.7 Partner Feedback Prep

This v0.7 pass prepares partner feedback handling after v0.6 governance. It is
a review-preparation layer only and does not add new conformance cases.

## Scope

- Confirm partner feedback remains categorized before action.
- Confirm partner-facing outputs remain summary-only and synthetic.
- Confirm complete private case content is not copied into partner packs.
- Confirm public paper support wording does not reveal private case content.
- Confirm real cooperation still requires NDA and de-identification review.

python tools/build_case_index.py

## Boundary

- no PHI
- no raw DICOM
- not clinical validation
- not regulatory approval
- not certification
- no fake DOI
- no real vendor confidential rules
- no public disclosure of private cases
- synthetic examples only
- FDO-style mapping only
- no robot operation evidence
