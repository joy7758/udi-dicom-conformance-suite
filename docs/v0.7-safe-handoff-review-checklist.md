# v0.7 Safe Handoff Review Checklist

Use this checklist before any customer-facing handoff discussion.

- Confirm `artifacts/safe-handoff-package-v0.6/` was regenerated and checked.
- Confirm the package manifest reports `phi_included=false`.
- Confirm the package manifest reports `raw_dicom_included=false`.
- Confirm the package manifest reports `real_customer_included=false`.
- Confirm the package manifest reports `real_hospital_included=false`.
- Confirm service governance audit reports `hits=[]`.
- Confirm customer feedback is routed through the v0.6 intake categories.
- Confirm any request for clinical validation, regulatory approval,
  certification, raw DICOM delivery, real hospital deployment, or real sample
  acceptance is marked out of scope.
- Confirm real cooperation requires NDA and de-identification review before
  sample-specific work begins.
