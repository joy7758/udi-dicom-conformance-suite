# Vendor Mapping Review Boundary

Vendor mapping review in this private suite is limited to synthetic examples and
declared profile behavior. It may compare partner-safe fixtures, expected error
codes, and FDO-style metadata mapping consistency.

It must not:

- encode real vendor-confidential rules
- copy private cases into partner packs
- use PHI
- use raw DICOM files
- claim clinical validation
- claim regulatory approval
- claim certification

When real vendor material is required, stop and route through a separate NDA and
data-handling review.
