# Expected Partner Questions v0.5

| Question | Safe Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Why do you require de-identified or synthetic samples? | To keep technical review separate from PHI handling and clinical operations. |
| Is the public validator a certification service? | No. It is a deterministic evidence consistency checker for review outputs. |
| What is the difference between partner pack and private suite? | The partner pack is summary-only and partner-safe; the private suite keeps internal cases. |
| Why use registry fixtures first? | Offline fixtures keep review deterministic; live lookup must be explicitly enabled. |
| What does FDO-style mapping mean? | It is optional metadata alignment, not an official FDO implementation claim. |
