| Item | Owner | Action | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  | technical review only |

Allowed actions: provide de-identified metadata, regenerate synthetic receipt,
clarify UDI-DI source, fix registry fixture, rerun redaction check.

claiming regulatory clearance, claiming certification, or promising hospital
deployment.
